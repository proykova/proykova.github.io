Mama, why are they killing us?

Synopsis

Dedicated to the innocent victims of this cruel war started by Russia on 24 February 2022, when it invaded a sovereign state – Ukraine.

Ordinary people on an ordinary morning suffered from a violent attack on their country. And everything changed forever. The people, their mornings, their land, their Ukraine. Ukrainian people, Ukrainian children and Ukrainian mothers. Everything! Glory to Ukraine! 💙💛
