Biography

Dima Proykova

Dima Proykova is a Bulgarian director, screenwriter, editor, photographer and actress living in Sofia.

Her work is distinguished by a feminist perspective and explores themes such as eroticism, fear and friendship, as well as discrimination based on gender, race, nationality, sexuality and other characteristics.

Education:

  • Art College of Screen Arts in 1994
  • National Academy of Theatre and Film Arts in 1998
  • Sofia University - Ecological Biotechnologies in 2014

She subsequently began making short fiction and documentary films, which have been screened at festivals across Europe, the United States, Africa and Asia.

Alongside her filmmaking, she maintains an ongoing photographic practice focused on cities at night, winter coastlines and life behind the scenes.

She also works as an editor on independent productions and as an actress in theatre and film.

Alongside her artistic work, she is also a biologist at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences.
